Squares in Squares

Pack n unit squares into the smallest square. Choose an n to explore constructions or try Game mode.

L is the larger horizontal or vertical span of the packing. Touching is legal; overlapping interiors are not. Empty board space and translation do not change L.

Each contribution is L / √n − 1; the competition score sums these for n = 1…1000. Lower is better. Proven cases invite you to match the bound.
